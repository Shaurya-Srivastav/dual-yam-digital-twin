# Research notes

- **Touch-only localisation.** A particle belief over object pose updated from contact / no-contact measurements, with the next probe chosen by expected information gain: [Memory Unscented Particle Filter](https://doi.org/10.1109/tro.2017.2707092), [information-gain criteria for active tactile selection](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2205.04697) (< 1 cm after 15 touches), [touch-only global localisation, ICRA 2026](https://doi.org/10.1109/icra57385.2026.11697596). Level 5 uses the hard-likelihood (set-membership) version of this belief; choosing pecks by information gain is not implemented yet.
- **Sensing a handle without moving it.** Set the contact threshold above sensor noise but below the breakaway force, pause on first contact, then estimate the joint from the contact pose: [contact-pause grasping](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.12969v2), [proprioceptive articulated-object estimation](https://tlips.be/revisiting-proprioception-for-articulated-manipulation/), [T-bar valve contact classification](https://kormushev.com/papers/Jamali_OCEANS-2013.pdf). Level 4 stops at ~0.9 N against ~1.5 N of swivel friction.
- **Flipping a cup with one arm.** Lift clear, rotate with full poses (orientation-only goals can drive the cup into the table), and use the wrist roll: practical single-arm guidance in the robot cup-stacking recipes found online; in-hand pivoting alternatives in [Gravitational Pivoting Using Tactile Sensing](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2210.05068). The roll attitude here was found by IK search, not taken from a paper.
